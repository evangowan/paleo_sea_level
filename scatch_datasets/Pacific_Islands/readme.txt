Blachon_Tahiti - A compilation of the data from Tahiti by Blanchon and Chutcharavan (2023), including some new data that provides more tight constraints. For the location of the Papeete cores, I used the locations reported in the Hibbert et al (2016) compilation. For the IODP cores, I used the locations from the IDOP reports. They use a 0.25 mm/yr subsidence rate to correct for tectonics. This was applied without uncertainty, and I have followed what they did. I am including datasets both with and without this correction. The dates from the Bard et al (1996,2010) dataset do not have paleowater depth estimates. Hibbert et al (2016) created (very large) water depth estimates which are not particularly useful as sea level indicators. I have set these data to be marine limiting. I have applied a ±0.5 m uncertainty to the marine limiting data.
