General note:
Bulk sediment, gyttja and peat dates are given a correction of 0±100 years to account for bulk effects.
=========
- Miyakojima - there is a script (Miyakojima_subaerial_age.sh) that calculates a terrestrial limiting point to account for the possible duration of subaerial exposure for the LGM corals found in Sasaki et al 2006.
- Kikaijima - I created two datasets, one using an assumed uplift rate of 1.9 mm/yr and the other at 2.1 mm/yr

Okinawa_beachrocks.ods - These data are all listed in Evelpidou et al 2019. The samples from Omoto (2007) do not have locations, so I have not included them. They indicated that the barnacles have an indicative menaing of the elevation +/- 0.2 m. For the beachrock, they used an indicative meaning of the mean high tide to mean low tide, which was 1.25 m. They included a 0.5 m elevation error. This means for barnacles it is 0.5 m, and for beachrock it is 0.8 m. One sample (N-2741) did not have a reference. It is in KobaEtal1982, and states it is a charcoal date, not a shell like in Evelpidou, so I have changed this.

Yoon_Nakdong_River.ods - These data are from the Nakdong River delta in Busan, South Korea. The dataset in the paper had some missing information. The calibrated radiocarbon dates from Yang et al 2024 needed to be taken from the original paper. The coordinates for the SB-14 core also needed to be retrieved from a map in Ryu et al 2005. The paper contains a lot of data that are marine and terrestrial limiting, but I am only including the sea level index points since they are well constrained and continuous through the Holocene.
